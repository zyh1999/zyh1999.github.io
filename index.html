Yihe Zhou

PhD Researcher

Understanding how learning works.
Making it more stable and efficient.

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of Manchester, supervised by Mingfei Sun and Samuel Kaski. I am a member of the Agent Lab.

During my master’s studies, I was supervised by Mingli Song.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning and optimisation. I study how the geometry of policy updates affects learning, with applications to language-model post-training and multi-agent decision-making.

RESEARCH

The geometry of learning

I am interested in methods that connect a clear mathematical view of learning with practical, reproducible algorithms.

Policy optimisation

Natural gradients, local curvature, and the relationship between parameter updates and changes in a policy.

LLM post-training

Stable reinforcement learning for language models, with attention to update geometry, numerical behaviour, and compute efficiency.

π

Multi-agent learning

Cooperation, credit assignment, and learning useful decentralised policies from centralised training or offline data.
